Profile

Third-year PhD candidate in Information Science at the Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology. My work studies how learning agents can acquire useful reward signals, explore efficiently, and create controllable structured content. Current interests include LLM-based reward generation, reinforcement learning, and neural methods for 3D botanical modeling.
