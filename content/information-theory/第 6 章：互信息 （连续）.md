# Chapter 6: Mutual Information: Continuous

## 6.1 Introduction / 引言

For continuous channels, probabilities are replaced by probability densities and sums by integrals. Mutual information remains the amount of uncertainty about one variable removed by observing another.

> 对连续信道，概率由概率密度取代，求和由积分取代。互信息仍表示观察一个变量后消除的关于另一个变量的不确定性。

**Figure 6.1. A noisy continuous channel transforms a signal into channel input \(X\), adds noise, and produces output \(Y\).**

> **图 6.1　有噪连续信道把信号转换为输入 \(X\)，加入噪声后产生输出 \(Y\)。**

## 6.2 Joint Distributions / 联合分布

The joint density \(p(x,y)\) describes how pairs of continuous values occur. It is normalised by

> 联合密度 \(p(x,y)\) 描述连续值对如何出现，并满足归一化条件：

$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}p(x,y)\,dx\,dy=1.$$

Marginal densities are obtained by integrating out the other variable:

> 对另一个变量积分即可得到边缘密度：

$$p(x)=\int p(x,y)\,dy,\qquad p(y)=\int p(x,y)\,dx.$$

If variables are independent,

> 如果变量相互独立：

$$p(x,y)=p(x)p(y).$$

Joint differential entropy is

> 联合微分熵为：

$$h(X,Y)=-\iint p(x,y)\log p(x,y)\,dx\,dy.$$

**Figures 6.2-6.4. Joint Gaussian densities for correlated and independent variables, shown as surfaces and bird's-eye density plots. Correlation tilts and narrows the cloud of likely pairs.**

> **图 6.2-6.4　相关与独立变量的联合高斯密度，以曲面和俯视密度图表示。相关性会使高概率值对形成倾斜而狭窄的云团。**

## 6.3 Conditional Distributions and Entropy / 条件分布与条件熵

The conditional density of \(Y\) given \(X=x\) is

> 已知 \(X=x\) 时，\(Y\) 的条件密度为：

$$p(y\mid x)=\frac{p(x,y)}{p(x)}.$$

For each fixed \(x\), it integrates to one. Conditional differential entropy averages the uncertainty of these slices:

> 对每个固定的 \(x\)，条件密度积分为 1。条件微分熵对这些切片的不确定性取平均：

$$h(Y\mid X)=\int p(x)h(Y\mid X=x)\,dx,$$
$$h(Y\mid X)=-\iint p(x,y)\log p(y\mid x)\,dx\,dy.$$

The chain rule is

> 链式法则为：

$$h(X,Y)=h(X)+h(Y\mid X)=h(Y)+h(X\mid Y).$$

**Figures 6.5-6.7. Slices through a joint density produce conditional densities \(p(Y\mid x_i)\) and \(p(X\mid y_i)\). Narrower slices mean less residual uncertainty.**

> **图 6.5-6.7　联合密度的切片产生条件密度 \(p(Y\mid x_i)\) 和 \(p(X\mid y_i)\)。切片越窄，剩余不确定性越小。**

## 6.4 Mutual Information and Conditional Entropy / 互信息与条件熵

For continuous variables,

> 对连续变量：

$$I(X;Y)=\iint p(x,y)\log\frac{p(x,y)}{p(x)p(y)}\,dx\,dy.$$

Equivalent entropy identities are

> 等价的熵恒等式为：

$$I(X;Y)=h(X)+h(Y)-h(X,Y),$$
$$I(X;Y)=h(X)-h(X\mid Y)=h(Y)-h(Y\mid X).$$

Although individual differential entropies may be negative or scale-dependent, mutual information is non-negative and has an operational meaning: the average reduction in uncertainty.

> 尽管单个微分熵可能为负或依赖尺度，互信息始终非负，并具有明确操作意义：它是平均不确定性的减少量。

**Figure 6.8. Mutual information is the overlap between the information represented by related continuous variables.**

> **图 6.8　互信息是相关连续变量所表示信息的重叠部分。**

## 6.5 Mutual Information is Invariant / 互信息的不变性

Let \(U=f(X)\) and \(V=g(Y)\) be invertible differentiable transformations. Jacobian terms alter \(h(X)\), \(h(Y)\), and \(h(X,Y)\), but cancel in their mutual-information combination:

> 设 \(U=f(X)\)、\(V=g(Y)\) 是可逆可微变换。雅可比项会改变 \(h(X)\)、\(h(Y)\) 和 \(h(X,Y)\)，但在互信息组合中相互抵消：

$$I(U;V)=I(X;Y).$$

Therefore changing units, rescaling axes, or applying any one-to-one reparameterisation does not alter shared information.

> 因此，改变单位、缩放坐标轴或进行任意一一重参数化，都不会改变共享信息量。

**Figure 6.9. Entropy components change under coordinate transformations, while their overlap, mutual information, remains fixed.**

> **图 6.9　坐标变换会改变各熵分量，但它们的重叠部分，即互信息，保持不变。**

## 6.6 Kullback-Leibler Divergence and Bayes / KL 散度与贝叶斯

The Kullback-Leibler divergence from density \(q\) to density \(p\) is

> 从密度 \(q\) 到密度 \(p\) 的 Kullback-Leibler 散度为：

$$D_{KL}(p\Vert q)=\int p(x)\log\frac{p(x)}{q(x)}\,dx\ge0.$$

It is zero only when the densities agree almost everywhere. It is not generally symmetric and is therefore not a metric.

> 只有当两个密度几乎处处相同时，KL 散度才为零。它通常不对称，因此不是距离度量。

Mutual information is a KL divergence:

> 互信息本身就是一种 KL 散度：

$$I(X;Y)=D_{KL}\bigl(p(x,y)\Vert p(x)p(y)\bigr).$$

Thus it measures how far the joint distribution is from independence. In Bayesian inference, the information gained from data \(D\) about parameter \(\theta\) can be measured by

> 因而它衡量联合分布偏离独立性的程度。在贝叶斯推断中，数据 \(D\) 关于参数 \(\theta\) 所提供的信息可以表示为：

$$D_{KL}\bigl(p(\theta\mid D)\Vert p(\theta)\bigr),$$

the divergence from prior to posterior. Averaging over possible data gives mutual information between parameters and observations.

> 即后验分布相对于先验分布的散度。对所有可能数据取平均，就得到参数与观测之间的互信息。

## 6.7 Summary / 小结

Joint and conditional densities extend probability theory to pairs of continuous variables. Continuous mutual information is a KL divergence between the joint density and the product of its marginals. It equals the reduction in differential entropy produced by observation, is always non-negative, and is invariant under invertible transformations.

> 联合密度和条件密度把概率理论扩展到连续变量对。连续互信息是联合密度与边缘密度乘积之间的 KL 散度，等于观察带来的微分熵减少量，始终非负，并在可逆变换下保持不变。
