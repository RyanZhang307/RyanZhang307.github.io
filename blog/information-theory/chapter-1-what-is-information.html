Chapter 01

什么是信息

What Is Information?

从眼睛、路线选择和二十问出发，建立信息、比特与二进制表示的直觉。

Most of the fundamental ideas of science are essentially simple, and may, as a rule, be expressed in a language comprehensible to everyone.

科学中的大多数基本思想，本质上都是简单的；一般说来，它们都可以用人人能够理解的语言表达。

Einstein A and Infeld L, 1938.

1.1 Introduction / 引言#

The universe is conventionally described in terms of physical quantities such as mass and velocity, but a quantity at least as important as these is information. Whether we consider computers30, evolution2,19, physics15, artificial intelligence9, quantum computation46, or the brain17,43, we are driven inexorably to the conclusion that their behaviours are largely determined by the way they process information.

按照传统，我们用质量、速度等物理量来描述宇宙；然而，至少还有一个与这些物理量同等重要的量，那就是信息。无论我们考察计算机30、进化2,19、物理学15、人工智能9、量子计算46还是大脑17,43，都会不可避免地得出一个结论：它们的行为在很大程度上取决于它们处理信息的方式。

Figure 1.1. Claude Shannon (1916-2001).

图 1.1　克劳德·香农（1916-2001）。

In 1948, Claude Shannon published a paper called A Mathematical Theory of Communication48. This paper heralded a transformation in our understanding of information. Before Shannon's paper, information had been viewed as a kind of poorly defined miasmic fluid. But after Shannon's paper, it became apparent that information is a well-defined and, above all, measurable quantity.

1948 年，克劳德·香农发表了论文《通信的数学理论》48。这篇论文预示着人类对信息的理解即将发生转变。在香农的论文发表之前，信息一直被视为一种定义不清、如同弥漫雾气般的东西。但在论文发表之后，人们认识到，信息是一种具有明确含义，而且最重要的是，可以测量的量。

Shannon's paper describes a subtle theory which tells us something fundamental about the way the universe works. However, unlike other great theories such as the Darwin-Wallace theory of evolution, information theory is not simple, and it is full of caveats. But we can disregard many of these caveats provided we keep a firm eye on the physical interpretation of information theory's defining equations. This will be our guiding principle in exploring the theory of information.

香农的论文描述了一套精妙的理论，它揭示了宇宙运行方式中的某些基本事实。不过，与达尔文-华莱士进化论等其他伟大理论不同，信息论并不简单，而且包含许多需要留意的限定条件。只要我们始终牢牢把握信息论定义方程的物理解释，就可以暂时略去其中许多限定条件。这将成为我们探索信息论时遵循的指导原则。

1.2 Information, Eyes and Evolution / 信息、眼睛与进化#

Shannon's theory of information provides a mathematical definition of information, and describes precisely how much information can be communicated between different elements of a system. This may not sound like much, but Shannon's theory underpins our understanding of how signals and noise are related, and why there are definite limits to the rate at which information can be communicated within any system, whether man-made or biological. It represents one of the few examples of a single theory creating an entirely new field of research. In this regard, Shannon's theory ranks alongside those of Darwin-Wallace, Newton, and Einstein.

香农的信息论为信息给出了数学定义，并精确描述了一个系统的不同组成部分之间可以传递多少信息。这听起来或许并不起眼，但我们之所以能够理解信号与噪声之间的关系，以及为什么任何系统，无论是人造系统还是生物系统，其信息传递速率都存在确定的上限，基础正是香农的理论。它是少数凭借单一理论开创全新研究领域的例子之一。就这一点而言，香农的理论可以与达尔文-华莱士、牛顿和爱因斯坦的理论并列。

When a question is typed into a computer search engine, the results provide useful information but it is buried in a sea of mostly useless data. In this internet age, it is easy for us to appreciate the difference between information and data, and we have learned to treat the information as a useful 'signal' and the rest as distracting 'noise'. This experience is now so commonplace that technical phrases like 'signal to noise ratio' are becoming part of everyday language. Even though most people are unaware of the precise meaning of this phrase, they have an intuitive grasp of the idea that 'data' means a combination of (useful) signals and (useless) noise.

当我们在计算机搜索引擎中输入一个问题时，搜索结果会提供有用的信息，但这些信息往往淹没在大量基本无用的数据之中。在互联网时代，我们很容易体会信息与数据之间的区别，也已经学会把信息看作有用的“信号”，把其余内容看作分散注意力的“噪声”。这种经验如今如此普遍，以至于“信噪比”之类的技术用语正在进入日常语言。尽管大多数人并不了解这个词的精确定义，却能直观地理解：“数据”是（有用的）信号和（无用的）噪声的组合。

The ability to separate signal from noise, to extract information from data, is crucial for modern telecommunications. For example, it allows a television picture to be compressed to its bare information bones and transmitted to a satellite, then to a TV, before being decompressed to reveal the original picture on the TV screen.

从噪声中分离信号、从数据中提取信息的能力，对现代通信至关重要。例如，我们可以把一幅电视图像压缩到只剩下最精简的信息“骨架”，将其发送至卫星，再传送到电视机，最后解压，在电视屏幕上还原原始图像。

This type of scenario is also ubiquitous in the natural world. The ability of eyes and ears to extract useful signals from noisy sensory data, and to package those signals efficiently, is the key to survival51. Indeed, the efficient coding hypothesis3,8,13,33 suggests that the evolution of sense organs, and of the brains that process data from those organs, is primarily driven by the need to minimise the energy expended for each bit of information acquired from the environment. More generally, a particular branch of brain science, computational neuroscience, relies on information theory to provide a benchmark against which the performance of neurons can be objectively measured.

这种情形在自然界中也无处不在。眼睛和耳朵能够从含噪声的感觉数据中提取有用信号，并以高效方式对这些信号进行编码，这是生存的关键51。事实上，高效编码假说3,8,13,33认为，感觉器官以及处理这些器官所产生数据的大脑之所以发生进化，主要动力是尽量减少从环境中获得每一比特信息所消耗的能量。更一般地说，脑科学的一个分支，即计算神经科学，依赖信息论提供客观衡量神经元性能的基准。

On a grander biological scale, the ability to separate signal from noise is fundamental to the Darwin-Wallace theory of evolution by natural selection12. Evolution works by selecting the individuals best suited to a particular environment so that, over many generations, information about the environment gradually accumulates within the gene pool. Thus, natural selection is essentially a means by which information about the environment is incorporated into DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid). And it seems likely that the rate at which information is incorporated into DNA is accelerated by an age-old biological mystery, sex. These and other applications of information theory are described in Chapter 9.

从更宏观的生物学尺度看，从噪声中分离信号的能力是达尔文-华莱士自然选择进化论的基础12。进化通过选择最适合特定环境的个体发挥作用，使有关环境的信息经过许多代逐渐积累到基因库中。因此，自然选择本质上是一种把环境信息写入 DNA（脱氧核糖核酸）的机制。而且，有一个古老的生物学谜题似乎会加快信息写入 DNA 的速度，那就是性。这些应用以及信息论的其他应用将在第 9 章介绍。

1.3 Finding a Route, Bit by Bit / 逐比特寻找路径#

Information is usually measured in bits, and one bit of information allows you to choose between two equally probable alternatives. The word bit is derived from binary digit (i.e. a zero or a one). However, as we shall see, bits and binary digits are fundamentally different types of entities.

信息通常以比特为单位进行测量，而一比特信息使你能够在两个等概率的备选项之间作出选择。英文单词 bit 源自 binary digit，即二进制数字（0 或 1）。不过，我们将会看到，比特与二进制数字在本质上是两类不同的实体。

Imagine you are standing at the fork in the road at point A in Figure 1.2, and that you want to get to the point marked D. Note that this figure represents a bird's-eye view, which you do not have; all you have is a fork in front of you, and a decision to make. If you have no prior information about which road to choose then the fork at A represents two equally probable alternatives. If I tell you to go left then you have received one bit of information. If we represent my instruction with a binary digit (0 = left and 1 = right) then this binary digit provides you with one bit of information, which tells you which road to choose.

假设你正站在图 1.2 中 A 点的岔路口，并且想要到达标记为 D 的地点。请注意，这幅图是你实际上看不到的鸟瞰图；你眼前只有一个岔路口，以及一个必须作出的决定。如果你事先完全不知道应该选择哪条路，那么 A 点的岔路就代表两个等概率的备选项。如果我告诉你向左走，你就获得了一比特信息。若用一个二进制数字表示我的指示（0 = 左，1 = 右），那么这个二进制数字就向你提供了一比特信息，告诉你应该选择哪条路。

Now imagine that you stroll on down the road and you come to another fork, at point B in Figure 1.2. Again, because you have no idea which road to choose, a binary digit (1 = right) provides one bit of information, allowing you to choose the correct road, which leads to the point marked C.

现在想象你沿路继续前行，来到图 1.2 中 B 点的另一个岔路口。你仍然不知道应该走哪条路，因此一个二进制数字（1 = 右）会提供一比特信息，使你能够选择通往 C 点的正确道路。

Note that C is one of four possible interim destinations that you could have reached after making two decisions. The two binary digits that allow you to make the correct decisions provided two bits of information, allowing you to choose from four (equally probable) possible alternatives; 4 happens to equal (2 \times 2 = 2^2).

请注意，C 是你作出两次决定后可能到达的四个中间目的地之一。帮助你作出正确决定的两个二进制数字共提供了两比特信息，使你能够从四个等概率的备选项中进行选择；而 (4 = 2 \times 2 = 2^2)。

A third binary digit (1 = right) provides you with one more bit of information, which allows you to again choose the correct road, leading to the point marked D.

第三个二进制数字（1 = 右）再提供一比特信息，使你又一次选择正确的道路，最终到达标记为 D 的地点。

Figure 1.2. How many roads must a man walk down? For a traveller who does not know the way, each fork in the road requires one bit of information to make a correct decision. The 0s and 1s on the right-hand side summarise the instructions needed to arrive at each destination; a left turn is indicated by a 0 and a right turn by a 1.

图 1.2　一个人究竟要走过多少条路？对于不认识道路的旅行者来说，每个岔路口都需要一比特信息才能作出正确决定。右侧的 0 和 1 概括了到达各个目的地所需的指令：0 表示左转，1 表示右转。

There are now eight roads you could have chosen from when you started at A, so three binary digits (which provide you with three bits of information) allow you to choose from eight equally probable alternatives; (8 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 2^3).

从 A 点出发时，你总共有八条最终道路可供选择。因此，三个二进制数字（向你提供三比特信息）使你能够从八个等概率的备选项中进行选择；(8 = 2 \times 2 \times 2 = 2^3)。

The decision taken at A excluded half of the eight possible destinations shown in Figure 1.2 that you could have arrived at. Similarly, the decision taken at each successive fork in the road halved the number of remaining possible destinations.

在 A 点作出的决定排除了图 1.2 所示八个可能目的地中的一半。同样，在此后每一个岔路口作出的决定，都会把剩余的可能目的地数量减半。

A Journey of Eight Alternatives / 八种备选项的旅程#

Let's summarise your journey in terms of the number of equally probable alternatives:

让我们按照等概率备选项的数量总结这段旅程：

  • If you have 1 bit of information then you can choose between 2 equally probable alternatives (i.e. (2^1 = 2)).
  • If you have 2 bits of information then you can choose between 4 equally probable alternatives (i.e. (2^2 = 4)).
  • Finally, if you have 3 bits of information then you can choose between 8 equally probable alternatives (i.e. (2^3 = 8)).
  • 如果你有 1 比特信息，就能在 2 个等概率备选项之间进行选择（即 (2^1 = 2)）。
  • 如果你有 2 比特信息，就能在 4 个等概率备选项之间进行选择（即 (2^2 = 4)）。
  • 最后，如果你有 3 比特信息，就能在 8 个等概率备选项之间进行选择（即 (2^3 = 8)）。

We can restate this in more general terms if we use (n) to represent the number of forks, and (m) to represent the number of final destinations. If you have come to (n) forks, then you have effectively chosen from

如果用 (n) 表示岔路口的数量，用 (m) 表示最终目的地的数量，就可以用更一般的方式重述这一结论。如果你经过了 (n) 个岔路口，那么你实际上是在如下数量的最终目的地中作出选择：

$$ m = 2^n \tag{1.1} $$

Because the decision at each fork requires one bit of information, (n) forks require (n) bits of information, which allow you to choose from (2^n) equally probable alternatives.

因为在每个岔路口作出决定都需要一比特信息，所以经过 (n) 个岔路口需要 (n) 比特信息，而这些信息使你能够从 (2^n) 个等概率备选项中进行选择。

There is a saying that "a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step". In fact, a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single decision: the direction in which to take the first step.

有句俗语说：“千里之行，始于足下。”事实上，千里之行始于一个决定：第一步应该朝哪个方向迈出。

Key point. One bit is the amount of information required to choose between two equally probable alternatives.

要点：一比特是从两个等概率备选项中作出选择所需要的信息量。

Binary Numbers / 二进制数#

We could label each of the eight possible destinations with a decimal number between 0 and 7, or with the equivalent binary number, as in Figure 1.2. These decimal numbers and their equivalent binary representations are shown in Table 1.1. Counting in binary is analogous to counting in decimal. Just as each decimal digit in a decimal number specifies how many 1s, 10s, 100s (etc.) there are, each binary digit in a binary number specifies how many 1s, 2s, 4s (etc.) there are. For example, the value of the decimal number 101 equals the number of 100s (i.e. (10^2)), plus the number of 10s (i.e. (10^1)), plus the number of 1s (i.e. (10^0)):

我们可以像图 1.2 那样，用 0 到 7 之间的十进制数标记八个可能目的地，也可以使用与之等价的二进制数。表 1.1 给出了这些十进制数及其对应的二进制表示。二进制计数与十进制计数类似。在十进制数中，每一位数字分别表示其中有多少个 1、10、100 等；同样，在二进制数中，每一位二进制数字分别表示其中有多少个 1、2、4 等。例如，十进制数 101 的值等于百位所表示的 100（即 (10^2)）加上十位所表示的 10（即 (10^1)）再加上个位所表示的 1（即 (10^0)）：

$$ (1 \times 100) + (0 \times 10) + (1 \times 1) = 101. \tag{1.2} $$

Similarly, the value of the binary number 101 equals the number of 4s (i.e. (2^2)), plus the number of 2s (i.e. (2^1)), plus the number of 1s (i.e. (2^0)):

同样，二进制数 101 的值等于其中的 4（即 (2^2)）加上其中的 2（即 (2^1)）再加上其中的 1（即 (2^0)）：

$$ (1 \times 4) + (0 \times 2) + (1 \times 1) = 5. \tag{1.3} $$

Binary / 二进制 000 001 010 011 100 101 110 111

Table 1.1. Decimal numbers and their equivalent binary representations.

表 1.1　十进制数及其对应的二进制表示。

The binary representation of numbers has many advantages. For instance, the binary number that labels each destination (e.g. 011) explicitly represents the set of left/right instructions required to reach that destination. This representation can be applied to any problem that consists of making a number of two-way (i.e. binary) decisions.

数的二进制表示具有许多优点。例如，标记每个目的地的二进制数（如 011）明确表示了到达该目的地所需的一系列左转和右转指令。这种表示方式可以应用于任何需要作出若干次二选一决定的问题。

Logarithms / 对数#

The complexity of any journey can be represented either as the number of possible final destinations or as the number of forks in the road which must be traversed in order to reach a given destination. We know that as the number of forks increases, so the number of possible destinations also increases. As we have already seen, if there are three forks then there are (8 = 2^3) possible destinations.

任何旅程的复杂程度，都可以用可能的最终目的地数量表示，也可以用到达某个目的地之前必须经过的岔路口数量表示。我们知道，岔路口越多，可能的目的地也越多。正如前面所见，如果有三个岔路口，就会有 (8 = 2^3) 个可能目的地。

Viewed from another perspective, if there are (m = 8) possible destinations then how many forks (n) does this imply? In other words, given eight destinations, what power of 2 is required in order to get 8? In this case, we know the answer is (n = 3), which is called the logarithm of 8. Thus, (3 = \log_2 8) is the number of forks implied by eight destinations.

换一个角度来看，如果有 (m = 8) 个可能目的地，那么这意味着存在多少个岔路口 (n)？换句话说，要得到 8，必须把 2 提高到多少次幂？在这个例子中，答案是 (n = 3)，3 被称为 8 的对数。因此，(3 = \log_2 8) 就是八个目的地所对应的岔路口数量。

More generally, the logarithm of (m) is the power to which 2 must be raised in order to obtain (m); that is, (m = 2^n). Equivalently, given a number (m) which we wish to express as a logarithm,

更一般地说，(m) 的对数，就是为了得到 (m) 而必须把 2 提高到的幂；也就是说，(m = 2^n)。等价地，对于希望以对数形式表示的数 (m)，有：

$$ n = \log_2 m. \tag{1.4} $$

The subscript 2 indicates that we are using logs to the base 2 (all logarithms in this book use base 2 unless stated otherwise). See Appendix C for a tutorial on logarithms.

下标 2 表示这里使用以 2 为底的对数。除非另有说明，本书中的所有对数都以 2 为底。关于对数的教程请参见附录 C。

A Journey of (\log_2(8)) Decisions / 一段包含 (\log_2(8)) 次决定的旅程#

Now that we know about logarithms, we can summarise your journey from a different perspective, in terms of bits:

了解对数以后，我们可以从另一个角度，按照比特数来总结这段旅程：

  • If you have to choose between 2 equally probable alternatives (i.e. (2^1)) then you need (1 (= \log_2 2^1 = \log_2 2)) bit of information.
  • If you have to choose between 4 equally probable alternatives (i.e. (2^2)) then you need (2 (= \log_2 2^2 = \log_2 4)) bits of information.
  • If you have to choose between 8 equally probable alternatives (i.e. (2^3)) then you need (3 (= \log_2 2^3 = \log_2 8)) bits of information.
  • 如果必须在 2 个等概率备选项（即 (2^1)）中进行选择，就需要 (1 (= \log_2 2^1 = \log_2 2)) 比特信息。
  • 如果必须在 4 个等概率备选项（即 (2^2)）中进行选择，就需要 (2 (= \log_2 2^2 = \log_2 4)) 比特信息。
  • 如果必须在 8 个等概率备选项（即 (2^3)）中进行选择，就需要 (3 (= \log_2 2^3 = \log_2 8)) 比特信息。

More generally, if you have to choose between (m) equally probable alternatives, then you need (n = \log_2 m) bits of information.

更一般地说，如果必须在 (m) 个等概率备选项中进行选择，就需要 (n = \log_2 m) 比特信息。

Key point. If you have (n) bits of information, then you can choose from (m = 2^n) equally probable alternatives. Equivalently, if you have to choose between (m) equally probable alternatives, then you need (n = \log_2 m) bits of information.

要点：如果拥有 (n) 比特信息，就可以从 (m = 2^n) 个等概率备选项中进行选择。等价地，如果必须在 (m) 个等概率备选项中进行选择，就需要 (n = \log_2 m) 比特信息。

1.4 A Million Answers to Twenty Questions / 二十个问题与百万种答案#

Navigating a series of forks in the road is, in some respects, similar to the game of '20 questions'. In this game, your opponent chooses a word (usually a noun), and you (the astute questioner) are allowed to ask 20 questions in order to discover the identity of this word. Crucially, each question must have a yes/no (i.e. binary) answer, and therefore provides you with a maximum of one bit of information.

在某些方面，穿过一连串岔路口与“二十个问题”游戏很相似。在这个游戏中，对手选定一个单词（通常是名词），而你这位敏锐的提问者可以提出 20 个问题，以确定这个单词是什么。关键在于，每个问题都必须得到“是”或“否”的回答，也就是二元回答，因此每个答案最多提供一比特信息。

By analogy with the navigation example, where each decision at a road fork halved the number of remaining destinations, each question should halve the number of remaining possible words. In doing so, each answer provides you with exactly one bit of information. A question to which you already know the answer is a poor choice of question. For example, if your question is, "Is the word in the dictionary?", then the answer is almost certainly, "Yes!", an answer which is predictable, and which therefore provides you with no information.

类比前面的导航例子，每次在岔路口作出决定都会把剩余目的地的数量减半，因此每个问题也应该把剩余的可能单词数量减半。做到这一点时，每个答案恰好提供一比特信息。如果你已经知道某个问题的答案，那么它就是一个糟糕的问题。例如，如果你问：“这个词在词典里吗？”答案几乎肯定是“在！”这个答案可以预料，因此不会向你提供任何信息。

Conversely, a well-chosen question is one to which you have no idea whether the answer will be yes or no; in this case, the answer provides exactly one bit of information. The cut-down version of '20 questions' in Figure 1.3 shows this more clearly.

相反，一个选择得当的问题，应当让你完全不知道答案会是“是”还是“否”；在这种情况下，答案恰好提供一比特信息。图 1.3 所示的“二十个问题”简化版更清楚地说明了这一点。

Figure 1.3. The game of '20 questions', here abbreviated to '3 questions'. Given an opponent who has one of eight words in mind, each yes/no question halves the number of remaining possible words. Each binary number on the right summarises the sequence of answers required to arrive at one word (no = 0 and yes = 1).

图 1.3　“二十个问题”游戏，这里缩减为“三个问题”。假设对手心中想的是八个单词之一，每个是/否问题都会把剩余的可能单词数量减半。右侧的每个二进制数概括了确定某个单词所需的一系列回答（否 = 0，是 = 1）。

In this game, your opponent has a vocabulary of exactly eight words, and you know which words they are. Your first question (Q1) could be, "Is it inanimate?", and the answer should halve the number of possible words to four, leading you to your second question (Q2). If your second question (Q2) is, "Is it a mammal?", then the answer should again halve the number of possible words, leading to your third question (Q3). By the time you arrive at Q3, there are just two possible words left, and after you have asked the third question (e.g. "Is it 'cat'?"), your opponent's yes/no response leads you to the correct answer. In summary, you have asked three questions, and excluded all but one out of eight possible words.

在这个游戏中，对手的词汇表恰好包含八个单词，而且你知道它们分别是什么。你的第一个问题 Q1 可以是：“它是无生命的吗？”答案应当把可能单词的数量减半为四个，然后你提出第二个问题 Q2。如果第二个问题是：“它是哺乳动物吗？”答案应当再次把可能单词的数量减半，然后进入第三个问题 Q3。到 Q3 时，只剩下两个可能单词；在提出第三个问题后，例如“它是‘猫’吗？”，对手的是/否回答就会把你引向正确答案。总之，你问了三个问题，并从八个可能单词中排除了除正确答案以外的全部单词。

More realistically, let's assume your opponent has the same vocabulary as you do (most of us have similar vocabularies, so this assumption is not entirely unreasonable). Specifically, let's assume this vocabulary contains exactly 1,048,576 words. Armed with this knowledge, each question can, in principle, be chosen to halve the number of remaining possible words. So, in an ideal world, your first question should halve the number of possible words to 524,288. Your next question should halve this to 262,144 words, and so on. By the time you get to the 19th question there should be just two words left, and after the 20th question, there should be only one word remaining.

更现实一些，我们假定对手和你拥有相同的词汇量。大多数人的词汇量相近，因此这个假设并非完全不合理。具体而言，假设这份词汇表恰好包含 1,048,576 个单词。知道这一点以后，原则上就可以选择每个问题，使剩余的可能单词数量减半。因此，在理想情况下，第一个问题应当把可能单词的数量减半为 524,288；下一个问题再将其减半为 262,144，依此类推。问到第 19 个问题时，应当只剩两个单词；回答第 20 个问题后，则只剩一个单词。

The reason this works out so neatly is because 20 questions allow you to choose from exactly (1{,}048{,}576 = 2^{20}) equally probable words (i.e. about one million). Thus, the 20 bits of information you have acquired with your questioning provide you with the ability to narrow down the range of possible words from about 1 million to just one. In other words, 20 questions allow you to find the correct word out of about a million possible words.

之所以能够得到如此整齐的结果，是因为 20 个问题使你能够从恰好 (1{,}048{,}576 = 2^{20}) 个等概率单词，也就是大约一百万个单词中进行选择。因此，通过提问获得的 20 比特信息，使你能够把可能单词的范围从大约一百万个缩小到一个。换句话说，20 个问题可以帮助你从大约一百万个可能单词中找出正确的那个。

Adding one more question would not only create a new game, '21 questions', it would also double the number of possible words (to about 2 million) that you could narrow down to one. By extension, each additional question allows you to acquire up to one more bit of information, and can therefore double the initial number of words. In principle, a game of '40 questions' allows you to acquire 40 bits of information, allowing you to find one out of (2^{40} \approx 10^{12}) words.

再增加一个问题，不仅会产生一种名为“二十一个问题”的新游戏，还会把你能够逐步缩小到唯一答案的初始单词数量翻倍到大约两百万个。依此类推，每增加一个问题，最多可以多获得一比特信息，因此可以把初始单词数量翻倍。原则上，“四十个问题”游戏可以让你获得 40 比特信息，从 (2^{40} \approx 10^{12}) 个单词中找出一个。

In terms of the navigation example, 40 bits would allow you to navigate 40 forks in the road, and would therefore permit you to choose one out of about a trillion possible routes. So the next time you arrive at your destination after a journey that involved 40 decisions, remember that you have avoided arriving at a trillion-minus-one incorrect destinations.

用导航的例子来说，40 比特信息可以帮助你通过 40 个岔路口，从而使你能够在大约一万亿条可能路线中选出一条。因此，下次当你经过一段涉及 40 次决定的旅程并到达目的地时，请记住，你成功避免了其他“一万亿减一”个错误目的地。

1.5 Information, Bits and Binary Digits / 信息、比特与二进制数字#

Despite the fact that the word bit is derived from binary digit, there is a subtle, but vital, difference between them. A binary digit is the value of a binary variable, where this value can be either a 0 or a 1, but a binary digit is not information per se. In contrast, a bit is a definite amount of information. Bits and binary digits are different types of entity, and to confuse one with the other is known as a category error.

尽管英文单词 bit 源自 binary digit，两者之间却存在一个细微而至关重要的区别。二进制数字是二元变量的取值，这个值可以是 0 或 1，但二进制数字本身并不等于信息。相比之下，比特表示一个确定的信息量。比特和二进制数字属于不同类型的实体，把其中一类与另一类混淆，称为“范畴错误”。

To illustrate this point, consider the following two extreme examples. At one extreme, if you already know that you should take the left-hand road from point A in Figure 1.2 and I show you the binary digit 0 (= left), then you have been given a binary digit but you have gained no information. At the other extreme, if you have no idea about which road to choose and I show you a 0, then you have been given a binary digit and you have also gained one bit of information. Between these extremes, if someone tells you there is a 71% probability that the left-hand road represents the correct decision and I subsequently confirm this by showing you a 0, then this 0 provides you with less than one bit of information (because you already had some information about which road to choose). In fact, when you receive my 0, you gain precisely half a bit of information (see Section 5.8). Thus, even though I cannot give you half a binary digit, I can use a binary digit to give you half a bit of information.

为了说明这一点，请考虑下面两个极端例子。在一个极端中，假设你已经知道在图 1.2 的 A 点应该选择左边的道路，而我向你展示二进制数字 0（= 左）。这时，你得到了一个二进制数字，却没有获得任何信息。在另一个极端中，假设你完全不知道该选择哪条路，而我向你展示 0。此时，你既得到了一个二进制数字，也获得了一比特信息。在这两个极端之间，假设有人告诉你左边道路是正确选择的概率为 71%，随后我用 0 确认了这一点。这个 0 向你提供的信息将少于一比特，因为你事先已经掌握了一些有关道路选择的信息。事实上，当你收到我给出的 0 时，获得的信息量恰好是半比特，参见第 5.8 节。因此，尽管我无法给你“半个二进制数字”，却可以利用一个二进制数字向你提供半比特信息。

The distinction between binary digits and bits is often ignored, with Pierce's book40 being a notable exception. Even some of the best textbooks use the terms 'bit' and 'binary digit' interchangeably. This does not cause problems for experienced readers as they can interpret the term 'bit' as meaning a binary digit or a bit's worth of information according to context. But for novices the failure to respect this distinction is a source of genuine confusion.

二进制数字与比特之间的区别经常被忽视，Pierce 的著作40是一个值得注意的例外。甚至一些最优秀的教科书也会交替使用“bit”和“binary digit”这两个术语。对经验丰富的读者来说，这通常不会造成问题，因为他们能够根据上下文判断“bit”究竟表示一个二进制数字，还是一比特的信息量。但对初学者而言，不尊重这一区别确实会造成混乱。

Sadly, in modern usage, the terms bit and binary digit have become synonymous, and MacKay (2003)34 proposed that the unit of information should be called the Shannon.

遗憾的是，在现代用法中，bit 和 binary digit 已经变成同义词，因此 MacKay（2003）34建议把信息量的单位称为“香农”（Shannon）。

Key point. A bit is the amount of information required to choose between two equally probable alternatives (e.g. left/right), whereas a binary digit is the value of a binary variable, which can adopt one of two possible values (i.e. 0/1).

要点：一比特是从两个等概率备选项（如左/右）中作出选择所需的信息量；二进制数字则是二元变量的取值，只能取两个可能值之一，即 0 或 1。

1.6 Example 1: Telegraphy / 例 1：电报#

Suppose you have just discovered that if you hold a compass next to a wire, then the compass needle changes position when you pass a current through the wire. If the wire is long enough to connect two towns like London and Manchester, then a current initiated in London can deflect a compass needle held near to the wire in Manchester.

假设你刚刚发现，把指南针放在导线旁边时，只要让电流通过导线，指南针的指针就会改变位置。如果导线长到足以连接伦敦和曼彻斯特这样的两座城市，那么在伦敦接通的电流，就可以使曼彻斯特导线旁的指南针发生偏转。

You would like to use this new technology to send messages in the form of individual letters. Sadly, the year is 1820, so you will have to wait over 100 years for Shannon's paper to be published. Undeterred, you forge ahead. Let's say you want to send only upper-case letters, to keep matters simple. So you set up 26 electric lines, one per letter from A to Z, with the first line being A, the second line being B, and so on. Each line is set up next to a compass which is kept some distance from all the other lines, to prevent each line from deflecting more than one compass.

你希望利用这项新技术，以单个字母的形式发送消息。遗憾的是，现在是 1820 年，你还要等一百多年，香农的论文才会发表。不过你并未因此退缩，而是继续尝试。为简单起见，假设只发送大写字母。于是，你铺设了 26 条电线，分别对应 A 到 Z：第一条线表示 A，第二条线表示 B，依此类推。每条电线旁边都放置一个指南针，并与其他电线保持一定距离，防止一条电线同时使多个指南针发生偏转。

In London, each line is labelled with a letter, and the corresponding line is labelled with the same letter in Manchester. For example, if you want to send the letter D, you press a switch on the fourth line in London, which sends an electric current to Manchester along the wire which is next to the compass labelled with the letter D. Of course, lines fail from time to time, and it is about 200 miles from London to Manchester, so finding the location of the break in a line is difficult and expensive. Naturally, if there were fewer lines then there would be fewer failures.

在伦敦，每条电线都标有一个字母；在曼彻斯特，对应电线也标有相同字母。例如，如果要发送字母 D，你就在伦敦按下第四条线路的开关。电流会沿电线传至曼彻斯特，使标有字母 D 的指南针发生偏转。当然，线路有时会发生故障，而伦敦到曼彻斯特相距约 200 英里，因此查找断线位置既困难又昂贵。显然，线路越少，故障也会越少。

With this in mind, Cooke and Wheatstone devised a complicated two-needle system, which could send only 23 different letters. Despite the complexity of their system, it famously led to the arrest of a murderer. On the first of January 1845, John Tawell poisoned his mistress, Sarah Hart, in a place called Salt Hill in the county of Berkshire, before escaping on a train to Paddington station in London. In order to ensure Tawell's arrest when he reached his destination, the following telegraph was sent to London:

考虑到这一点，Cooke 和 Wheatstone 设计了一套复杂的双指针系统，但它只能发送 23 个不同字母。尽管系统十分复杂，却因协助逮捕一名杀人犯而闻名。1845 年 1 月 1 日，John Tawell 在伯克郡一个名为 Salt Hill 的地方毒死了他的情妇 Sarah Hart，随后乘火车逃往伦敦帕丁顿车站。为了确保 Tawell 抵达目的地时即被逮捕，人们向伦敦发送了如下电报：

A MURDER HAS GUST BEEN COMMITTED AT SALT HILL AND THE SUSPECTED MURDERER WAS SEEN TO TAKE A FIRST CLASS TICKET TO LONDON BY THE TRAIN WHICH LEFT SLOUGH AT 742 PM HE IS IN THE GARB OF A KWAKER ...

SALT HILL 刚刚发生一起谋杀案，有人看见嫌疑犯购买了头等车票，乘坐晚上 7 点 42 分从 SLOUGH 开出的火车前往伦敦。他身穿贵格会教徒的服装……

The unusual spellings of the words JUST and QUAKER were a result of the telegrapher doing his best in the absence of the letters J, Q and Z in the array of 23 letters before him. As a result of this telegram, Tawell was arrested and subsequently hanged for murder. The role of Cooke and Wheatstone's telegraph in Tawell's arrest was widely reported in the press, and established the practicality of telegraphy.

电文把 JUST 拼成 GUST、把 QUAKER 拼成 KWAKER，是因为电报员面前的 23 个字母中没有 J、Q 和 Z，只能尽力用其他字母代替。Tawell 因这封电报被捕，后来因谋杀罪被处以绞刑。新闻界广泛报道了 Cooke-Wheatstone 电报系统在逮捕 Tawell 过程中发挥的作用，从而确立了电报技术的实用价值。

In the 1830s, Samuel Morse and Alfred Vail developed the first version of what came to be known as Morse code. Because this specified each letter as dots and dashes, it could be used to send messages over a single line.

19 世纪 30 年代，Samuel Morse 和 Alfred Vail 开发了后来被称为摩尔斯电码的第一个版本。由于它用点和划表示每个字母，因此只用一条线路就能发送消息。

An important property of Morse code is that it uses short codewords for the most common letters, and longer codewords for less common letters, as shown in Table 1.2.

摩尔斯电码的一个重要性质是：常见字母使用较短的码字，不常见字母使用较长的码字，如表 1.2 所示。

Morse adopted a simple strategy to find out which letters were most common. Reasoning that newspaper printers would have only as many copies of each letter as were required, he went to a printer's workshop and counted the copies of each letter. As a result, the most common letter E is specified as a single dot, whereas the rare J is specified as a dot followed by three dashes.

Morse 采用了一种简单策略来确定哪些字母最常见。他认为，报纸印刷商只会按实际需要准备各种字母的铅字，因此便到印刷工坊统计每个字母的铅字数量。结果，最常见的字母 E 用一个点表示，而罕见字母 J 则用一个点加三条划表示。

The ingenious strategy adopted by Morse is important because it enables efficient use of the communication channel (a single wire). We will return to this theme many times, and it raises a fundamental question: how can we tell if a communication channel is being used as efficiently as possible?

Morse 采用的巧妙策略之所以重要，是因为它能够高效利用通信信道，也就是那一条电线。我们将多次回到这个主题，而它提出了一个根本问题：怎样判断一个通信信道是否已经得到尽可能高效的利用？

1.7 Example 2: Binary Images / 例 2：二值图像#

The internal structure of most images is highly predictable. For example, most of the individual picture elements or pixels in the image of stars in Figure 1.4 are black, with an occasional white pixel, a star. Because almost all pixels are black, it follows that most pairs of adjacent pixels are also black, which makes the image's internal structure predictable. If this picture were taken by the orbiting Hubble telescope then its predictable structure would allow it to be efficiently transmitted to Earth.

大多数图像的内部结构都具有很强的可预测性。例如，在图 1.4 的星空图像中，绝大多数单个图像元素，也就是像素，都是黑色的，只偶尔出现一个表示恒星的白色像素。由于几乎所有像素都是黑色，相邻像素对大多也都是黑色，因此图像的内部结构可以预测。假如这幅图像由在轨运行的哈勃望远镜拍摄，那么这种可预测的结构就能让图像高效地传回地球。

Suppose you were in charge of writing the computer code which conveys the information in Figure 1.4 from the Hubble telescope to Earth. You could naively send the value of each pixel; let's call this method A. Because there are only two values in this particular image (black and white), you could choose to indicate the colour black with the binary digit 0, and the colour white with a 1. You would therefore need to send as many 0s and 1s as there are pixels in the image. For example, if the image was (100 \times 100) pixels then you would need to send ten thousand 0s or 1s for the image to be reconstructed on Earth. Because almost all the pixels are black, you would send sequences of hundreds of 0s interrupted by the occasional 1. It is not hard to see that this is a wasteful use of the expensive satellite communication channel. How could it be made more efficient?

假设你负责为哈勃望远镜编写计算机代码，把图 1.4 中的信息传回地球。最直接但较为简单粗暴的做法，是逐个发送每个像素的值，我们称之为方法 A。由于这幅图像中只有黑、白两个取值，可以用二进制数字 0 表示黑色，用 1 表示白色。因此，图像有多少个像素，就需要发送多少个 0 和 1。例如，如果图像大小为 (100 \times 100) 像素，为了在地球上重建它，就必须发送一万个 0 或 1。由于几乎所有像素都是黑色，实际发送的将是数百个连续的 0，只偶尔被一个 1 打断。不难看出，这种做法浪费了昂贵的卫星通信信道。怎样才能提高效率呢？

Another method consists of sending only the locations of the white pixels (method B). This would yield a code like ([(19,13), (22,30), \ldots]), where each pair of numbers represents the row and column of a white pixel.

另一种方法是只发送白色像素的位置，我们称之为方法 B。这样会得到类似 ([(19,13), (22,30), \ldots]) 的编码，其中每一对数字表示一个白色像素所在的行和列。

Figure 1.4. The night sky. Each pixel contains one of just two values.

图 1.4　夜空。每个像素只有两个可能取值之一。

Yet another method consists of concatenating all of the rows of the image, and then sending the number of black pixels that occur before the next white pixel (method C). If the number of black pixels that precede the first white pixel is 13 and there are 9 pixels before the next white pixel, then the first row of the image begins with 000000000000010000000001..., and the code for communicating this would be [13,9,...], which is clearly more compact than the 24 binary digits which begin the first row of the image.

还有一种方法，是把图像的所有行首尾连接起来，然后发送每个白色像素之前出现的黑色像素数量，我们称之为方法 C。如果第一个白色像素之前有 13 个黑色像素，而下一个白色像素之前又有 9 个黑色像素，那么图像的第一行将以 000000000000010000000001... 开头，而传递这部分内容所用的编码将是 [13,9,...]。显然，这比直接发送第一行开头的 24 个二进制数字更加紧凑。

Notice that method A consists of sending the image itself, whereas methods B and C do not send the image, but they do send all of the information required to reconstruct the image on Earth. Crucially, the end results of all three methods are identical, and it is only the efficiency of the methods that differs.

请注意，方法 A 发送的是图像本身，而方法 B 和 C 并不直接发送图像，却会发送在地球上重建图像所需的全部信息。关键在于，三种方法得到的最终结果完全相同，区别只在于效率。

In fact, whether A, B, or C is the most efficient method depends on the structure of the image. This can be seen if we take an extreme example consisting of just one white pixel in the centre of the image. For this image, method A is fairly useless, because it would require 10,000 binary values to be sent. Method B would consist of two numbers, ((50,50)), and method C would consist of a single number, 5,050. If we ignore the brackets and commas then we end up with four decimal digits for both methods B and C. So these methods seem to be equivalent, at least for the example considered here.

实际上，A、B、C 中哪一种方法效率最高，取决于图像的结构。考虑一个极端例子就能看出这一点：图像中央只有一个白色像素。对这幅图像而言，方法 A 几乎毫无用处，因为它需要发送 10,000 个二进制值。方法 B 只包含两个数 ((50,50))，方法 C 则只包含一个数 5,050。如果忽略括号和逗号，那么方法 B 和 C 都只需要四个十进制数字。因此，至少在这个例子中，两种方法似乎是等效的。

For other images, with other structures, different encoding methods will be more or less efficient. For example, Figure 1.5 contains just two grey-levels, but these occur in large regions of pure black or pure white. In this case, it seems silly to use method B to send the location of every white pixel, because so many of them occur in long runs of white pixels. This observation makes method C seem to be an obvious choice - but with a slight change. Because there are roughly equal numbers of black and white pixels which occur in regions of pure black or pure white, we could just send the number of pixels which precede the next change from black to white or from white to black. This is known as run-length encoding.

对于具有其他结构的图像，不同编码方法的效率会各有高低。例如，图 1.5 只包含两个灰度级，但它们分别集中在大片纯黑或纯白区域中。在这种情况下，如果采用方法 B，逐个发送每个白色像素的位置，显然并不合理，因为大量白色像素会连续成片出现。这个观察表明，稍加修改的方法 C 是一个自然选择。由于黑、白像素的数量大致相等，而且它们分别集中在纯黑或纯白区域，我们只需发送从当前位置到下一次黑白转换或白黑转换之前的像素数量。这种方法称为游程编码。

Figure 1.5. In a binary image, each pixel has 1 out of 2 possible grey-levels.

图 1.5　在二值图像中，每个像素具有两个可能灰度级之一。

To illustrate this, if the distance from the first black pixel in the middle row to the first white pixel (the girl's hair) is 87 pixels, and the distance from there to the next black pixel is 31 pixels, and the distance to the next white pixel is 18 pixels, then this part of the image would be encoded as [87,31,18,...]. Provided we know the method used to encode an image, it is a relatively simple matter to reconstruct the original image from the encoded image.

举例来说，如果在中间一行中，从第一个黑色像素到第一个白色像素，也就是女孩头发的位置，距离为 87 个像素；从那里到下一个黑色像素的距离为 31 个像素；再到下一个白色像素的距离为 18 个像素，那么图像的这一部分可以编码为 [87,31,18,...]。只要知道图像采用的编码方法，就可以较为容易地从编码结果重建原始图像。

1.8 Example 3: Grey-Level Images / 例 3：灰度图像#

Suppose we wanted to transmit an image of (100 \times 100) pixels, in which each pixel has more than two possible grey-level values. A reasonable number of grey-levels turns out to be 256, as shown in Figure 1.6a. As before, there are large regions that look as if they contain only one grey-level. In fact, each such region contains grey-levels which are similar, but not identical, as shown in Figure 1.7. The similarity between nearby pixel values means that adjacent pixel values are not independent of each other, and that the image has a degree of redundancy. How can this observation be used to encode the image?

假设我们希望传输一幅 (100 \times 100) 像素的图像，其中每个像素可以具有两个以上的灰度值。实践表明，256 个灰度级是一个合理的数量，如图 1.6a 所示。与前面的情况一样，图中有许多大片区域看起来似乎只包含一个灰度级。实际上，如图 1.7 所示，每个这样的区域都包含彼此相近但并不完全相同的灰度级。附近像素值的相似性意味着相邻像素值并不相互独立，也说明图像存在一定程度的冗余。怎样利用这一观察来编码图像呢？

Figure 1.6. Grey-level image. (a) An image in which each pixel has one out of 256 possible grey-levels, between 0 and 255, each of which can be represented by a binary number with 8 binary digits (e.g. (255 = 11111111)). (b) Histogram of grey-levels in the picture.

图 1.6　灰度图像。（a）每个像素具有 0 到 255 之间的 256 个可能灰度级之一，每个灰度级都可以用一个 8 位二进制数表示，例如 (255 = 11111111)。（b）图像灰度级的直方图。

One method consists of encoding the image in terms of the differences between the grey-levels of adjacent pixels. For brevity, we will call this difference coding. More complex methods exist, but most are similar in spirit to this simple method. In principle, pixel differences could be measured in any direction within the image, but, for simplicity, we concatenate consecutive rows to form a single row of 10,000 pixels, and then take the difference between adjacent grey-levels. We can see the result of difference coding by 'un-concatenating' the rows to reconstitute an image, as shown in Figure 1.8a, which looks like a badly printed version of Figure 1.6a. As we shall see, both images contain the same amount of information.

一种方法是按照相邻像素灰度级之差对图像进行编码。为简洁起见，我们把它称为差分编码。尽管还存在更复杂的方法，但大多数方法的基本思想都与这种简单方法相似。原则上，可以沿图像中的任意方向测量像素差值。为简单起见，我们把连续各行首尾连接，形成一个包含 10,000 个像素的单行序列，然后计算相邻灰度级之间的差。把这些行重新拆开并恢复成图像后，就能看到差分编码的结果，如图 1.8a 所示。它看起来像图 1.6a 的低质量印刷版本。我们将会看到，两幅图像包含的信息量相同。

If adjacent pixel grey-levels in a given row are similar, then the difference between the grey-levels is close to zero. In fact, a histogram of difference values shown in Figure 1.8b shows that the most common difference values are indeed close to zero, and only rarely greater than (\pm 63). Thus, using difference coding, we could represent almost every one of the 9,999 difference values in Figure 1.8a as a number between (-63) and (+63).

如果同一行中相邻像素的灰度级相近，那么二者的灰度差就接近于零。图 1.8b 所示的差值直方图证实，最常见的差值确实接近零，而且差值很少超过 (\pm 63)。因此，采用差分编码时，图 1.8a 中 9,999 个差值几乎都可以表示为 (-63) 到 (+63) 之间的数。

Figure 1.7. Adjacent pixels tend to have similar grey-levels, so the image has a large amount of redundancy, which can be used for efficient encoding. (a) Grey-level image. (b) Magnified square from a. (c) Magnified square from b, with individual pixel grey-levels indicated.

图 1.7　相邻像素往往具有相近的灰度级，因此图像中存在大量可以用于高效编码的冗余。（a）灰度图像。（b）图 a 中方框区域的放大图。（c）图 b 中方框区域的放大图，并标出各个像素的灰度级。

In those rare cases where the grey-level difference is larger than (\pm 63), we could list these separately as each pixel's location (row and column, encoded as (2 \times 7) binary digits), and its grey-level (8 binary digits). Most coding procedures have special 'housekeeping' fragments of computer code to deal with things like this, but these account for a negligible percentage of the total storage space required. For simplicity, we will assume that this percentage is zero.

对于少数灰度差大于 (\pm 63) 的情况，可以单独列出每个像素的位置，包括用 (2 \times 7) 个二进制数字表示的行和列，以及用 8 个二进制数字表示的灰度级。大多数编码程序都包含专门处理这类情况的“辅助管理”代码片段，但它们占总存储空间的比例可以忽略不计。为简单起见，我们假设这一比例为零。

At first, it is not obvious how difference coding represents any saving over simply sending the value of each pixel's grey-level. However, because these differences are between (-63) and (+63), they span a range of 127 different values, i.e. ([-63,-62,\ldots,0,\ldots,62,63]). Any number in this range can be represented using seven binary digits, because (7 = \log_2 128), leaving one spare value.

乍看之下，差分编码相对于直接发送每个像素的灰度级，似乎并没有节省空间。但由于这些差值位于 (-63) 到 (+63) 之间，它们总共覆盖 127 个不同的值，即 ([-63,-62,\ldots,0,\ldots,62,63])。这个范围内的任何数都可以用七个二进制数字表示，因为 (7 = \log_2 128)，并且还会剩余一个未使用的值。

In contrast, if we were to send each pixel's grey-level in Figure 1.6a individually, then we would need to send 10,000 grey-levels. Because each grey-level could be any value between 0 and 255, we would have to send eight binary digits ((8 = \log_2 256)) for each pixel.

相比之下，如果逐个发送图 1.6a 中每个像素的灰度级，就需要发送 10,000 个灰度值。由于每个灰度级都可能是 0 到 255 之间的任意值，每个像素都必须发送八个二进制数字，因为 (8 = \log_2 256)。

Once we have encoded an image into 9,999 pixel grey-level differences ((d_1, d_2, \ldots, d_{9999})), how do we use them to reconstruct the original image? If the difference (d_1) between the first pixel grey-level (x_1) and the second pixel grey-level (x_2) is, say, (d_1 = (x_2 - x_1) = 10) grey-levels and the grey-level of (x_1) is 5, then we obtain the original grey-level of (x_2) by adding 10 to (x_1); that is, (x_2 = x_1 + d_1), so (x_2 = 5 + 10 = 15). We then continue this process for the third pixel ((x_3 = x_2 + d_2)), and so on. Thus, provided we know the grey-level of the first pixel in the original image, which can be encoded as eight binary digits, we can use the pixel grey-level differences to recover the grey-level of every pixel in the original image.

把一幅图像编码为 9,999 个像素灰度差 ((d_1, d_2, \ldots, d_{9999})) 后，怎样利用这些差值重建原始图像呢？假设第一个像素的灰度级 (x_1) 与第二个像素的灰度级 (x_2) 之差为 (d_1 = (x_2 - x_1) = 10)，而 (x_1) 的灰度级为 5，那么把 10 加到 (x_1) 上，就可以得到 (x_2) 的原始灰度级。也就是说，(x_2 = x_1 + d_1)，所以 (x_2 = 5 + 10 = 15)。接下来对第三个像素继续同样的过程，即 (x_3 = x_2 + d_2)，依此类推。因此，只要知道原始图像第一个像素的灰度级，而这个灰度级可以用八个二进制数字编码，就能利用像素灰度差恢复原始图像中每个像素的灰度级。

Figure 1.8. Difference coding. (a) Each pixel grey-level is the difference between adjacent horizontal grey-level values in Figure 1.6a (grey = zero difference). (b) Histogram of grey-level differences between adjacent pixel grey-levels in Figure 1.6a. Only differences between (\pm 63) are plotted.

图 1.8　差分编码。（a）每个像素的灰度级表示图 1.6a 中水平方向相邻灰度值之差，灰色表示差值为零。（b）图 1.6a 中相邻像素灰度差的直方图。图中只绘出了 (\pm 63) 范围内的差值。

The fact that we can reconstruct the original image (Figure 1.6a) from the grey-level differences (Figure 1.8a) proves that they both contain exactly the same amount of information.

我们可以从灰度差图像（图 1.8a）重建原始图像（图 1.6a），这一事实证明两者包含的信息量完全相同。

Let's work out the total saving from using this difference coding method. The naive method of sending all pixel grey-levels, which vary between 0 and 255, would need eight binary digits per pixel, requiring a total of 80,000 binary digits. Using difference coding we would need seven binary digits per difference value, making a total of 70,000 binary digits. Therefore, using difference coding provides a saving of 10,000 binary digits, or 12.5%.

下面计算使用这种差分编码方法总共可以节省多少空间。直接发送所有像素灰度级的简单方法中，灰度值介于 0 到 255 之间，每个像素需要八个二进制数字，总计需要 80,000 个二进制数字。采用差分编码时，每个差值需要七个二进制数字，总计需要 70,000 个二进制数字。因此，差分编码可以节省 10,000 个二进制数字，也就是 12.5%。

In practice, a form of difference coding is used to reduce the amount of data required to transmit voices over the telephone, where it is known as differential pulse code modulation. Using the differences between consecutive values, a voice signal which would otherwise require eight binary digits per value can be transmitted with just five binary digits.

实际上，电话语音传输会采用一种差分编码形式来减少所需数据量，这种技术称为差分脉冲编码调制。通过使用连续取值之间的差，原本每个取值需要八个二进制数字的语音信号，只需五个二进制数字就能传输。

As we shall see in subsequent chapters, a histogram of data values (e.g. image grey-levels) can be used to find an upper bound for the average amount of information each data value could convey. Accordingly, the histogram (Figure 1.6b) of the grey-levels in Figure 1.6a defines an upper bound of 7.84 bits/pixel. In contrast, the histogram (Figure 1.8b) of the grey-level differences in Figure 1.8a defines an upper bound of just 5.92 bits/pixel.

后续章节将会说明，数据值的直方图，例如图像灰度级直方图，可以用来求出每个数据值平均能够传递的信息量上界。因此，图 1.6a 的灰度级直方图（图 1.6b）给出的上界是 7.84 比特/像素。相比之下，图 1.8a 的灰度差直方图（图 1.8b）给出的上界只有 5.92 比特/像素。

Given that the images in Figures 1.6a and 1.8a contain the same amount of information, and that Figure 1.8a contains no more than 5.92 bits/pixel, it follows that Figure 1.6a cannot contain more than 5.92 bits/pixel either. This matters because Shannon's work guarantees that if each pixel's grey-level contains an average of 5.92 bits of information, then we should be able to represent Figure 1.6a using no more than 5.92 binary digits per pixel. But this still represents an upper bound. In fact, the smallest number of binary digits required to represent each pixel is equal to the amount of information, measured in bits, implicit in each pixel. So what we really want to know is: how much information does each pixel contain?

图 1.6a 和图 1.8a 包含相同的信息量，而图 1.8a 每个像素包含的信息不超过 5.92 比特，因此图 1.6a 每个像素包含的信息也不可能超过 5.92 比特。这一点非常重要，因为香农的工作保证：如果每个像素的灰度级平均包含 5.92 比特信息，那么我们应该能够用不超过 5.92 个二进制数字表示图 1.6a 中的每个像素。不过，这仍然只是一个上界。实际上，表示每个像素所需的最少二进制数字数量，等于该像素所隐含的信息量，以比特计。因此，我们真正想知道的是：每个像素究竟包含多少信息？

This is a hard question, but we can get an idea of the answer by comparing the amount of computer memory required to represent the image in two different contexts. For simplicity, we assume that each pixel has eight binary digits. First, in order to display the image on a computer screen, the value of each pixel occupies eight binary digits, so the bigger the picture, the more memory it requires to be displayed. Second, a compressed version of the image can be stored on the computer's hard drive using an average of less than eight binary digits per pixel, for example by using the difference coding method above. Consequently, storing the compressed version of an image on the hard drive requires less memory than displaying that image on the screen. In practice, image files are usually stored in compressed form with the method used to compress the image indicated by the file name extension, for example .jpeg.

这是一个困难的问题，但通过比较在两种不同情境下表示图像所需的计算机内存，可以大致了解答案。为简单起见，假设每个像素用八个二进制数字表示。第一，为了在计算机屏幕上显示图像，每个像素的值占用八个二进制数字，因此图像越大，显示它所需的内存就越多。第二，压缩后的图像可以存储在计算机硬盘上，平均每个像素使用不到八个二进制数字，例如采用前述差分编码方法。因此，把图像的压缩版本存储在硬盘上，所需空间少于在屏幕上显示该图像所需的内存。实际中，图像文件通常以压缩形式存储，文件扩展名会表明所采用的压缩方法，例如 .jpeg。

The image in Figure 1.6a is actually 344 by 299 pixels, where each pixel grey-level is between 0 and 255, which can be represented as eight binary digits (because (2^8 = 256)), or one byte. This amounts to a total of 102,856 pixels, each of which is represented on a computer screen as one byte. However, when the file containing this image is inspected, it is found to contain only 45,180 bytes; the image in Figure 1.6a can be compressed by a factor of (2.28 (= 102856/45180)) without any loss of information. This means that the information implicit in each pixel, which requires eight binary digits for it to be displayed on a screen, can be represented with about four binary digits on a computer's hard drive.

图 1.6a 的实际大小是 344 × 299 像素。每个像素的灰度级介于 0 到 255 之间，可以用八个二进制数字表示，因为 (2^8 = 256)，也就是一个字节。图像总计包含 102,856 个像素，在计算机屏幕上显示时，每个像素用一个字节表示。然而，检查包含这幅图像的文件后会发现，其大小只有 45,180 字节。图 1.6a 可以在不损失任何信息的情况下压缩 (2.28 (= 102856/45180)) 倍。这意味着，每个像素所隐含的信息，在屏幕上显示时需要八个二进制数字，而在计算机硬盘上只需大约四个二进制数字就能表示。

Thus, even though each pixel can adopt any one of 256 possible grey-levels, and is displayed using eight binary digits of computer memory, the grey-level of each pixel can be stored in about four binary digits. This is important, because it implies that each set of eight binary digits used to display each pixel in Figure 1.6a contains an average of only four bits of information, and therefore each binary digit contains only half a bit of information. At first sight, this seems like an odd result. But we already know from Section 1.5 that a binary digit can represent half a bit, and we shall see later, especially in Chapter 5, that a fraction of a bit is a well-defined quantity which has a reasonably intuitive interpretation.

因此，尽管每个像素可以取 256 个可能灰度级中的任意一个，并在计算机内存中用八个二进制数字显示，但每个像素的灰度级只需大约四个二进制数字就能存储。这一点非常重要，因为它意味着，用于显示图 1.6a 中每个像素的每组八个二进制数字，平均只包含四比特信息；因此，每个二进制数字平均只包含半比特信息。乍看之下，这个结果似乎很奇怪。但我们已经从第 1.5 节知道，一个二进制数字确实可以表示半比特信息。后面，尤其是在第 5 章中，我们还会看到，比特的小数部分是一个定义明确、并且具有相当直观解释的量。

1.9 Summary / 小结#

From navigating a series of forks in the road, and playing the game of '20 questions', we have seen how making binary choices requires information in the form of simple yes/no answers. These choices can also be used to choose from a set of letters, and can therefore be used to send typed messages along telegraph wires.

通过穿过一系列岔路口以及进行“二十个问题”游戏，我们看到，作出二元选择需要以简单的是/否答案形式提供信息。这类选择还可以用于从一组字母中进行选择，因此可以用来通过电报线发送文字消息。

We found that increasing the number of choices from two (forks in the road) to 26 (letters) to 256 (pixel grey-levels) allowed us to transmit whole images down a single wire as a sequence of binary digits. In each case, the redundancy of the data in a message allowed it to be compressed before being transmitted. This redundancy emphasises a key point: a binary digit does not necessarily provide one bit of information. More importantly, a binary digit is not the same type of entity as a bit of information.

我们发现，把选择数量从两个（岔路方向）增加到 26 个（字母），再增加到 256 个（像素灰度级），就可以把整幅图像表示为二进制数字序列，通过一条线路传输。在每种情况下，消息中数据的冗余都使其能够在传输前得到压缩。这种冗余突出说明了一个关键事实：一个二进制数字不一定提供一比特信息。更重要的是，二进制数字与信息比特并不是同一类型的实体。

So, what is information? It is what remains after every iota of natural redundancy has been squeezed out of a message, and after every aimless syllable of noise has been removed. It is the unfettered essence that passes from computer to computer, from satellite to Earth, from eye to brain, and, over many generations of natural selection, from the natural world to the collective gene pool of every species.

那么，什么是信息？当消息中每一丝天然冗余都被压榨出去、噪声中每一个毫无意义的音节都被去除后，剩下的就是信息。它是一种摆脱束缚的精华，从一台计算机传到另一台计算机，从卫星传到地球，从眼睛传到大脑，并且经过许多代自然选择，从自然界进入每个物种的共同基因库。
