Chapter 09

信息即大自然的货币

Information as Nature’s Currency

信息论在压缩、进化、基因组、神经系统与感知中的应用。

9.1 Introduction / 引言#

Information is not confined to telecommunications. It is stored in genomes, transformed by evolution, encoded by sensory systems, transmitted by neurons, and exploited by technology. Across these domains, the same questions recur: how much information is present, how efficiently is it represented, and what limits its transmission?

信息并不局限于电信领域。它存储在基因组中，经由进化改变，被感觉系统编码，由神经元传递，并被各种技术利用。在这些领域中，同样的问题反复出现：存在多少信息、信息表示有多高效、传输受到什么限制？

9.2 Satellite TVs, MP3 and All That / 卫星电视、MP3 等技术#

Modern communication systems combine the two coding theorems. Source coding removes predictable redundancy to reduce data rate; channel coding adds carefully structured redundancy so that transmission errors can be detected and corrected.

现代通信系统结合两条编码定理。信源编码删除可预测冗余，以降低数据率；信道编码加入经过精心设计的结构化冗余，以检测和纠正传输错误。

Digital television compresses spatial and temporal similarities between pixels and frames, then protects the compressed bitstream against satellite noise. MP3 coding exploits properties of hearing: components masked by louder sounds can be represented coarsely or discarded. This is lossy compression, but perceptual information is preserved well enough that the result sounds similar to the original.

数字电视压缩像素之间和连续画面之间的空间、时间相似性，再保护压缩后的比特流免受卫星信道噪声影响。MP3 编码利用听觉特性：被较强声音掩蔽的成分可以粗略表示或直接丢弃。这属于有损压缩，但与感知相关的信息得到充分保留，因此听感接近原始声音。

Compression, encryption and error correction serve different purposes. Compression removes redundancy, encryption hides statistical structure from an adversary, and error correction introduces redundancy that is useful to a decoder.

压缩、加密和纠错具有不同目的。压缩删除冗余；加密向对手隐藏统计结构；纠错则引入对解码器有用的冗余。

9.3 Does Sex Accelerate Evolution? / 性是否加速进化？#

Natural selection accumulates information about the environment in a population's gene pool. Better-adapted genetic variants leave more descendants, so environmental constraints become statistically represented in genomes.

自然选择把关于环境的信息积累到种群基因库中。适应性更好的遗传变体留下更多后代，因此环境约束逐渐以统计方式表现在基因组中。

Asexual reproduction passes linked genes together. Beneficial mutations arising in different individuals compete and cannot easily be combined. Sexual recombination can place beneficial variants from different parents into the same offspring, explore genotype combinations more rapidly, and remove harmful combinations.

无性繁殖把相互连锁的基因整体传递。不同个体中出现的有利突变会彼此竞争，难以组合。性重组能够把不同亲本的有利变体集中到同一后代中，更快探索基因型组合，并清除有害组合。

In idealised models, the amount of environmental information accumulated can grow with population variation and the number of independently recombining loci. Sex does not guarantee faster adaptation in every environment, because it also breaks up successful combinations and has biological costs, but it can greatly increase the rate at which useful information is assembled.

在理想化模型中，所积累的环境信息量可以随种群变异和独立重组位点数量增加。性并不保证在所有环境中都加速适应，因为它也会拆散成功组合并具有生物成本；但它能够显著提高有用信息组合起来的速度。

图 9.1　无性与有性种群的适合度随世代变化，展示重组在何种条件下加速适应。

9.4 The Human Genome: How Much Information? / 人类基因组包含多少信息？#

Each DNA base is one of four symbols, A, C, G and T. If all were independent and equiprobable, each would carry (\log_2 4=2) bits. A haploid human genome of roughly three billion base pairs would then have a raw upper bound near six billion bits, about 750 megabytes.

每个 DNA 碱基是 A、C、G、T 四种符号之一。如果它们相互独立且等概率，每个碱基携带 (\log_2 4=2) 比特。约含 30 亿碱基对的单倍体人类基因组，其原始上界约为 60 亿比特，即约 750 MB。

Actual information is lower because bases are not equiprobable or independent, repeated sequences are abundant, two chromosome copies are similar, and large regions can be predicted from evolutionary relatives. Conversely, biological meaning cannot be estimated merely by counting protein-coding bases: regulatory sequences, structural organisation and context also matter.

实际信息量更低，因为碱基并非等概率或相互独立，重复序列很多，两套染色体相似，而且大量区域可以根据进化近缘物种预测。另一方面，也不能只统计蛋白质编码碱基来衡量生物意义：调控序列、结构组织和上下文同样重要。

Information content is distinct from functional importance. A short control sequence may contain few bits yet have a large effect on development.

信息量不同于功能重要性。一段很短的控制序列也许只含少量比特，却可能对发育产生巨大影响。

9.5 Enough DNA to Wire Up a Brain? / DNA 足以连接大脑吗？#

The human brain contains roughly (10^{11}) neurons and perhaps (10^{14}) or more synapses. Explicitly listing every connection would require far more information than the genome appears to contain.

人脑约含 (10^{11}) 个神经元和可能达到 (10^{14}) 以上的突触。若明确列出每一条连接，需要的信息远超过基因组看似能够容纳的数量。

The genome therefore specifies generative rules rather than a complete wiring diagram. Chemical gradients, cell types, local growth rules, activity-dependent plasticity and experience collectively build the network. A compact developmental program can generate a structure whose detailed state contains much more data than the program itself.

因此，基因组规定的是生成规则，而不是完整接线图。化学梯度、细胞类型、局部生长规则、活动依赖可塑性和经验共同构建神经网络。紧凑的发育程序可以生成一个细节状态所含数据远多于程序本身的结构。

This is analogous to a short algorithm generating a complex image or the digits of (\pi): description length can be much smaller than the generated object.

这类似于短算法生成复杂图像或 (\pi) 的数字：描述长度可以远小于生成对象本身。

9.6 Are Brains Good at Processing Information? / 大脑擅长处理信息吗？#

Neurons communicate with voltage spikes. Dividing time into short bins turns a spike train into a binary sequence: 1 if a spike occurs, 0 otherwise.

神经元通过电压脉冲进行通信。把时间划分为短时间箱，就能把脉冲序列转换为二进制序列：出现脉冲记为 1，否则记为 0。

Figure 9.2. A sequence of neuronal spikes represented as binary values in successive time intervals.

图 9.2　把神经元脉冲序列表示为连续时间区间中的二进制值。

If firing rate is (r) spikes/s and bin width is (\Delta t), the approximate spike probability is (p=r\Delta t), with (q=1-p). Entropy per bin is

若放电率为 (r) 个脉冲/秒，箱宽为 (\Delta t)，脉冲概率近似为 (p=r\Delta t)，而 (q=1-p)。每个时间箱的熵为：

$$H_{\mathrm{bin}}=p\log\frac1p+q\log\frac1q.$$

For small (\Delta t), the approximate information rate is related to

当 (\Delta t) 很小时，近似信息速率与下式有关：

$$R\approx r\log_2\frac1{r\Delta t}.$$

For (r=50) spikes/s and (\Delta t=1) ms, the chapter obtains about 216 bits/s, or 4.32 bits/spike. Real neurons are constrained by refractory periods, correlations, metabolic cost and noise, so empirical information must be estimated from stimulus-response distributions rather than firing rate alone.

当 (r=50) 个脉冲/秒、(\Delta t=1) ms 时，本章得到约 216 比特/秒，即 4.32 比特/脉冲。真实神经元受不应期、相关性、代谢成本和噪声限制，因此经验信息量必须根据刺激-响应分布估计，而不能只看平均放电率。

Efficient Sensory Coding / 高效感觉编码#

Sensory systems adapt their response functions to natural stimulus statistics. Laughlin's experiment on fly photoreceptors found that neural responses approximately equalised the frequency of output values. A cumulative-distribution transform

感觉系统会让响应函数适应自然刺激统计。Laughlin 对苍蝇光感受器的实验发现，神经响应近似均衡了输出值的出现频率。累积分布变换：

$$g(s)=\int_{-\infty}^{s}p_S(u),du$$

maps a non-uniform stimulus distribution into an approximately uniform bounded response, maximising output entropy.

能把非均匀刺激分布映射为近似均匀的有界响应，从而最大化输出熵。

Figure 9.3. Laughlin's experiment compares natural contrast statistics, photoreceptor responses and the information-optimal encoding function.

图 9.3　Laughlin 实验比较自然对比度统计、光感受器响应和信息最优编码函数。

Human cone photoreceptors have overlapping wavelength sensitivities, so their raw outputs are correlated. Subsequent neural circuits approximately recode them into luminance and opponent-colour channels, such as sums and differences, reducing redundancy.

人类视锥细胞的波长敏感曲线相互重叠，因此原始输出彼此相关。后续神经回路把它们近似重新编码为亮度通道和颜色拮抗通道，例如和与差，从而降低冗余。

Figures 9.4-9.6. Cone sensitivities and sum-difference recoding illustrate how correlated receptor outputs are transformed into less redundant channels.

图 9.4-9.6　视锥敏感曲线以及和-差重编码，展示相关的感受器输出如何转换为冗余更低的信道。

Brains are not designed solely to maximise bits. They must represent behaviourally relevant variables, operate quickly, remain robust and minimise energy use. Efficient coding is therefore one important objective among several.

大脑并非只为最大化比特数而设计。它必须表示与行为相关的变量、快速运行、保持稳健并减少能耗。因此，高效编码只是多个重要目标之一。

9.7 A Very Short History of Information Theory / 信息论极简史#

Telegraphy and coding problems motivated early quantitative ideas. Hartley linked information to the logarithm of the number of possible messages. Nyquist studied transmission rates. During the Second World War, cryptanalysis sharpened statistical approaches to communication.

电报和编码问题推动了早期定量思想。Hartley 把信息与可能消息数量的对数联系起来；Nyquist 研究传输速率；第二次世界大战期间，密码分析进一步发展了通信的统计方法。

Claude Shannon unified these strands in his 1948 paper A Mathematical Theory of Communication, defining entropy, channel capacity and the coding theorems. Subsequent work developed practical compression, error correction, cryptography, statistical inference, neuroscience, genetics and quantum information.

Claude Shannon 在 1948 年论文《通信的数学理论》中统一了这些线索，定义熵、信道容量和编码定理。后续研究发展出实用压缩、纠错、密码学、统计推断、神经科学、遗传学和量子信息。

9.8 Summary / 小结#

Information acts as a common currency across technology and nature. Communication systems trade redundancy against compression and reliability. Evolution stores environmental information in genomes; developmental rules expand compact genetic descriptions into complex organisms. Nervous systems transform natural stimulus statistics into useful, relatively efficient neural codes. The same entropy and mutual-information concepts illuminate all these cases, while physical and biological constraints determine what “efficient” means in practice.

信息是贯穿技术与自然界的共同通货。通信系统在压缩、冗余和可靠性之间权衡；进化把环境信息存储到基因组中；发育规则把紧凑遗传描述展开为复杂生物体；神经系统把自然刺激统计转换为有用且相对高效的神经编码。熵和互信息概念能够统一解释这些现象，而物理与生物约束决定实践中“高效”的具体含义。
