Appendix A: Glossary / 附录 A：术语表#

Term English explanation 中文术语 中文解释
alphabet The set of symbols a variable or message may use. 字母表 / 符号集 一个变量或消息可以使用的全部符号所组成的集合。
average A representative central value. For observations, it is the sample mean; for a distribution, it is the expected value. 平均值 表示数据中心位置的数值。对样本是算术平均，对分布是期望值。
Bayes' rule A rule that reverses a conditional probability by combining likelihood, prior and marginal likelihood. 贝叶斯规则 结合似然、先验概率和边缘似然，以反转条件概率的规则。

discrete Able to take values from a countable set. 离散的 只能从可数集合中取值的。

Appendix C: Logarithms / 附录 C：对数#

A logarithm states the power to which a base must be raised. Since (10^2=100), (log_{10}100=2). The inverse operation is exponentiation: if (y=log_a x), then (x=a^y).

对数表示底数必须提升到的幂次。因为 (10^2=100)，所以 (log_{10}100=2)。其逆运算是乘方：若 (y=log_a x)，则 (x=a^y)。

Logarithms are useful because they turn multiplication into addition:

对数的重要作用是把乘法转换为加法：

$$ \log_b(nm)=\log_b n+\log_b m, \tag{C.6} $$

$$ nm=b^{\log_b n+\log_b m}. \tag{C.7} $$

The change-of-base rule is

换底公式为：

$$ \log_b x=\frac{\log_a x}{\log_a b}. \tag{C.8} $$

Natural logarithms have base (e) and are written (ln x). Unless otherwise stated, the book uses base-2 logarithms. Figure C.1 compares the monotonic logarithmic functions with bases 2 and 10.

自然对数以 (e) 为底，写作 (ln x)。除非另有说明，本书采用以 2 为底的对数。图 C.1 比较了以 2 和 10 为底的单调对数函数。

Appendix D: Probability Density Functions / 附录 D：概率密度函数#

A function maps values from a domain to a range. A probability density function (pdf) maps every possible value of a continuous variable to a non-negative density. Probability is area, not the height at a single point.

函数把定义域中的值映射到值域。概率密度函数把连续变量的每个可能取值映射为非负密度。概率对应曲线下面积，而不是某一点的曲线高度。

A histogram approximates a pdf. Divide observations into (M) bins of width (Delta x), let (a_i) be the area of bin (i), and let (A) be the total area:

直方图可以近似概率密度函数。把观测值分入 (M) 个宽度为 (Delta x) 的箱，令 (a_i) 为第 (i) 个箱的面积，(A) 为总面积：

$$ A=\sum_{i=1}^{M}a_i. \tag{D.1} $$

The normalised bin probabilities sum to 1, and the width of a bin is

归一化后的各箱概率之和为 1，箱宽为：

$$ \sum_{i=1}^{M}P_i=1, \qquad \Delta x=x_i^{\max}-x_i^{\min}. \tag{D.2-D.3} $$

For bin (i), probability equals area and density equals area divided by width:

对第 (i) 个箱，概率等于面积，而密度等于面积除以宽度：

$$ P_i=p(x_i)\Delta x, \qquad p(x_i)=\frac{P_i}{\Delta x}. \tag{D.4-D.6} $$

As (Delta x) approaches zero, the sum becomes an integral. A valid pdf satisfies

当 (Delta x) 趋于零时，求和变为积分。有效的概率密度函数满足：

$$ \sum_{i=1}^{M}p(x_i)\Delta x\longrightarrow \int_{\mathcal X}p(x),dx=1. \tag{D.7-D.8} $$

Figure D.1 shows a normalised histogram approaching a smooth pdf as bins become thinner. Figure D.2 shows that the probability of an interval is the area between its boundaries and the pdf curve.

图 D.1 展示归一化直方图如何在箱变窄时逼近平滑的概率密度函数。图 D.2 展示某区间的概率就是该区间边界与密度曲线围成的面积。

Appendix E: Averages From Distributions / 附录 E：从分布求平均值#

For a discrete variable, the mean, average and expected value are the probability-weighted sum of possible values:

对离散变量，均值、平均值和期望值都是各可能取值按概率加权后的总和：

$$ E[X]=\sum_{i=1}^{m}p(x_i)x_i. \tag{E.1} $$

For example, values (4,5,6) with probabilities (0.1,0.2,0.7) have expectation

例如，取值 (4,5,6) 的概率分别为 (0.1,0.2,0.7)，其期望为：

$$ E[X]=(0.1)(4)+(0.2)(5)+(0.7)(6)=5.6. \tag{E.2-E.5} $$

More generally, the expected value of a function (h(X)) is

更一般地，函数 (h(X)) 的期望为：

$$ E[h(X)]=\sum_{i=1}^{m}p(x_i)h(x_i). \tag{E.6-E.9} $$

If (h(x)=\log_2[1/p(x)]), this expectation is entropy:

若 (h(x)=\log_2[1/p(x)])，这个期望就是熵：

$$ E[h(X)]=\sum_i p(x_i)\log_2\frac{1}{p(x_i)}=H(X). \tag{E.10-E.11} $$

For a continuous variable, summation is replaced by integration:

对连续变量，求和由积分代替：

$$ E[h(X)]=\int_{\mathcal X}p(x)h(x),dx. \tag{E.12} $$

Choosing Shannon information for (h(x)) gives differential entropy:

令 (h(x)) 为香农信息量，即得到微分熵：

$$ H(X)=\int_{\mathcal X}p(x)\log_2\frac{1}{p(x)},dx. \tag{E.13-E.14} $$

Appendix F: The Rules of Probability / 附录 F：概率规则#

Independent Outcomes / 独立结果#

If two outcomes are independent, their joint probability is the product of their individual probabilities:

若两个结果彼此独立，其联合概率等于各自概率的乘积：

$$ p(x,y)=p(x)p(y). $$

For a coin with (p(h)=0.9) and (p(t)=0.1), two flips give (p(h,h)=0.81), (p(t,t)=0.01), and a head and tail in either order with probability (0.18). Table F.1 expresses the expected counts out of 100 pairs and distinguishes ordered sequences ((h,t)) from unordered combinations ({h,t}).

对一枚 (p(h)=0.9)、(p(t)=0.1) 的硬币，连续投掷两次得到双正面的概率为 (0.81)，双反面的概率为 (0.01)，一正一反而不计顺序的概率为 (0.18)。表 F.1 用 100 对投掷的期望计数说明这些结果，并区分有序序列 ((h,t)) 与无序组合 ({h,t})。

Conditional Probability and Product Rule / 条件概率与乘法规则#

$$ p(x\mid y)=\frac{p(x,y)}{p(y)}, \tag{F.4} $$

$$ p(x,y)=p(x\mid y)p(y). \tag{F.5} $$

The vertical bar is read as "given". The product rule follows directly by multiplying the conditional-probability definition by (p(y)).

竖线读作“给定”。把条件概率定义的两边乘以 (p(y))，就得到乘法规则。

Sum Rule and Marginalisation / 求和规则与边缘化#

For a discrete variable,

对离散变量：

$$ p(x)=\sum_i p(x,y_i)=\sum_i p(x\mid y_i)p(y_i). \tag{F.6-F.7} $$

For a continuous variable,

对连续变量：

$$ p(x)=\int p(x,y),dy=\int p(x\mid y)p(y),dy. \tag{F.8} $$

This operation removes (Y) from the joint distribution and produces the marginal probability of (X).

这个运算从联合分布中消去 (Y)，得到 (X) 的边缘概率。

Bayes' Rule / 贝叶斯规则#

Because (p(y,x)=p(x,y)), the two product-rule expressions can be equated:

因为 (p(y,x)=p(x,y))，可以令乘法规则的两种写法相等：

$$ p(y\mid x)p(x)=p(x\mid y)p(y). \tag{F.9-F.10} $$

Therefore,

因此：

$$ p(y\mid x)=\frac{p(x\mid y)p(y)}{p(x)}. \tag{F.11} $$

In Bayesian terminology, (p(y\mid x)) is the posterior, (p(x\mid y)) the likelihood, (p(y)) the prior, and (p(x)) the marginal likelihood or evidence. The same relation applies to complete distributions:

在贝叶斯术语中，(p(y\mid x)) 是后验概率，(p(x\mid y)) 是似然，(p(y)) 是先验概率，(p(x)) 是边缘似然或证据。同一关系也适用于完整分布：

$$ p(Y\mid X)=\frac{p(X\mid Y)p(Y)}{p(X)}. \tag{F.12} $$

Appendix G: The Gaussian Distribution / 附录 G：高斯分布#

A Gaussian probability density function, also called a normal distribution or bell curve, with mean (mu) and standard deviation (sigma) is

均值为 (mu)、标准差为 (sigma) 的高斯概率密度函数，也称正态分布或钟形曲线：

$$ p(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}\exp\left[-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right]. \tag{G.1} $$

Writing

令：

$$ z=\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}, \tag{G.2} $$

gives (p(x)=ke^{-z}), where (k=1/(\sigma\sqrt{2\pi})). The density is greatest at (x=\mu). Changing (mu) shifts the curve, while increasing (sigma) spreads it out. Figure G.1(a) shows the standard Gaussian with (mu=0) and (sigma=1).

于是 (p(x)=ke^{-z})，其中 (k=1/(\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}))。密度在 (x=\mu) 处最大。改变 (mu) 会平移曲线，增大 (sigma) 会使曲线更分散。图 G.1(a) 展示 (mu=0,sigma=1) 的标准高斯分布。

The Central Limit Theorem / 中心极限定理#

As the number of observations in each sample grows, the distribution of sample means drawn from almost any parent distribution becomes increasingly Gaussian. Many physical quantities are affected by multiple factors and are therefore weighted sums or averages, which helps explain the prevalence of Gaussian distributions in nature.

随着每个样本中的观测数量增大，从几乎任意母分布得到的样本均值分布会越来越接近高斯分布。许多物理量受多个因素共同影响，实质上是加权和或平均，这有助于解释高斯分布为何在自然界中普遍出现。

Cumulative Distribution Function / 累积分布函数#

The Gaussian cumulative distribution function is the area under the pdf from negative infinity to (x):

高斯累积分布函数是从负无穷到 (x) 的概率密度曲线下面积：

$$ \Phi(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}\int_{-\infty}^{x} \exp\left[-\frac{(t-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right]dt. \tag{G.3} $$

Thus (Phi(x)=P(X\leq x)). Figure G.1(b) plots this cumulative function for the standard Gaussian.

因而 (Phi(x)=P(X\leq x))。图 G.1(b) 绘出了标准高斯分布的累积分布函数。

Appendix H: Key Equations / 附录 H：关键公式#

Entropy / 熵#

For a discrete random variable,

对离散随机变量：

$$ H(X)=\sum_{i=1}^{m}p(x_i)\log_2\frac{1}{p(x_i)}. \tag{H.1} $$

For a continuous random variable,

对连续随机变量：

$$ H(X)=\int_{\mathcal X}p(x)\log_2\frac{1}{p(x)},dx. \tag{H.2} $$

Joint Entropy / 联合熵#

$$ H(X,Y)=\sum_{i=1}^{m}\sum_{j=1}^{n}p(x_i,y_j) \log_2\frac{1}{p(x_i,y_j)}, \tag{H.3} $$

$$ H(X,Y)=\int_{\mathcal X}\int_{\mathcal Y}p(x,y) \log_2\frac{1}{p(x,y)},dy,dx. \tag{H.4} $$

The information diagram identity is

信息图中的恒等式为：

$$ H(X,Y)=I(X,Y)+H(X\mid Y)+H(Y\mid X). \tag{H.5} $$

Conditional Entropy / 条件熵#

$$ H(Y\mid X)=\sum_i\sum_j p(x_i,y_j) \log_2\frac{1}{p(y_j\mid x_i)}, \tag{H.6} $$

$$ H(X\mid Y)=\sum_i\sum_j p(x_i,y_j) \log_2\frac{1}{p(x_i\mid y_j)}. \tag{H.7} $$

For continuous variables, replace the sums by integrals:

对连续变量，把求和替换为积分：

$$ H(X\mid Y)=\int!!\int p(x,y)\log_2\frac{1}{p(x\mid y)},dx,dy, \tag{H.8} $$

$$ H(Y\mid X)=\int!!\int p(x,y)\log_2\frac{1}{p(y\mid x)},dx,dy. \tag{H.9} $$

Equivalent identities and the chain rule are

等价恒等式与熵的链式法则为：

$$ H(X\mid Y)=H(X,Y)-H(Y), \tag{H.10} $$

$$ H(Y\mid X)=H(X,Y)-H(X), \tag{H.11} $$

$$ H(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y\mid X)=H(Y)+H(X\mid Y). \tag{H.12} $$

Mutual Information / 互信息#

$$ I(X,Y)=\sum_i\sum_j p(x_i,y_j) \log_2\frac{p(x_i,y_j)}{p(x_i)p(y_j)}, \tag{H.13} $$

$$ I(X,Y)=\int!!\int p(x,y) \log_2\frac{p(x,y)}{p(x)p(y)},dx,dy. \tag{H.14} $$

Equivalent forms are

等价形式为：

$$ I(X,Y)=H(X)+H(Y)-H(X,Y), \tag{H.15} $$

$$ I(X,Y)=H(X)-H(X\mid Y), \tag{H.16} $$

$$ I(X,Y)=H(Y)-H(Y\mid X), \tag{H.17} $$

$$ I(X,Y)=H(X,Y)-[H(X\mid Y)+H(Y\mid X)]. \tag{H.18} $$

Channel Capacity / 信道容量#

Channel capacity is the largest mutual information attainable by choosing the input distribution:

信道容量是通过选择输入分布所能达到的最大互信息：

$$ C=\max_{p(X)}I(X,Y). \tag{H.19} $$

Marginalisation / 边缘化#

For discrete variables,

对离散变量：

$$ p(x_i)=\sum_j p(x_i,y_j), \qquad p(y_j)=\sum_i p(x_i,y_j). \tag{H.20-H.21} $$

For continuous variables,

对连续变量：

$$ p(x)=\int_{\mathcal Y}p(x,y),dy, \qquad p(y)=\int_{\mathcal X}p(x,y),dx. \tag{H.22} $$
